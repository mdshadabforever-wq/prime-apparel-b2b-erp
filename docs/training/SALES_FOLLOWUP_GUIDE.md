# Sales Follow‑up Team Guide

## 📌 Department Purpose
- **Responsibility:** Follow up with qualified leads, manage order negotiations, close sales.
- **Goal:** Convert leads into confirmed orders and maintain healthy relationships.
- **Business Importance:** Direct revenue driver; impacts cash flow and inventory planning.

## 🔐 Login Instructions
1. Open the ERP URL and sign in with corporate credentials.
2. After authentication you land on the **Sales Dashboard**.
3. Role `SALES_FOLLOWUP_TEAM` displays only sales‑related menus (Leads, Orders, Customer Timeline).

## 📅 Daily Workflow
1. **Review Assigned Leads** – Go to **Leads → Assigned**.
2. **Contact Lead** – Click **Call/Email**, log interaction notes.
3. **Create Order** – When ready, click **New Order**, select customer, add line items, set delivery date.
4. **GST & Pricing Check** – System auto‑applies GST and pricing rules.
5. **Confirm & Send Invoice** – Approve order, system generates GST‑validated invoice PDF.
6. **Update CRM** – Add follow‑up tasks, mark lead status (Negotiation, Won, Lost).

## 🛠️ Real‑Life Example
- Follow up with “Metro Fashion” lead from Buyer Hunting.
- Negotiate 2,000 units of cotton shirts at ₹1300 each.
- Create order #SO‑2026‑045, set delivery 14 days.
- System adds GST ₹10 % and generates invoice.
- Mark lead as **Won** and schedule production.

## 🚫 Mistakes To Avoid
- **Missing GST validation** – leads to invoice rejection.
- **Incorrect delivery date** – can cause stock shortages.
- **Not updating CRM** – future team loses context.

## ✅ SOP Checklist (Daily)
- [ ] Review leads assigned to you.
- [ ] Log all communications in CRM.
- [ ] Create orders for qualified leads.
- [ ] Verify GST and pricing before confirming.
- [ ] Export and send invoices.
- [ ] Update lead status.

## 📞 Escalation Flow
- **Pricing dispute** → Notify **Pricing Team**.
- **GST issue** → Contact **Technical Team**.
- **Urgent order** → Alert **Founder/Admin**.

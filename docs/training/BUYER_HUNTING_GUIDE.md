# Buyer Hunting Team Guide

## 📌 Department Purpose
- **Responsibility:** Identify and qualify potential buyers, conduct outreach, and add them to the CRM.
- **Goal:** Grow the buyer pipeline with high‑quality leads.
- **Business Importance:** Fuels the sales funnel and ensures continuous order inflow.

## 🔐 Login Instructions
1. Log in to the ERP URL with your corporate email/password.
2. After authentication you land on the **Buyer Hunting Dashboard**.
3. Role `BUYER_HUNTING_TEAM` shows only lead‑generation menus.

## 📅 Daily Workflow
1. **Lead Research** – Navigate to **Leads → New Research**.
2. **Add Lead** – Click **Add Lead**, fill:
   - Company name, contact person, phone, email, GSTIN.
   - System validates GST status in real‑time.
3. **Assign Category** – Tag the lead (e.g., *Large Retailer*, *Boutique*).
4. **Enrich Data** – Attach notes, past interactions, and potential order volume.
5. **Submit** – Lead appears in **Leads → Pending** for the Sales Follow‑up team.

## 🛠️ Real‑Life Example
- Research “Urban Fashion Co.”, verify GSTIN `27AAEPM1234F1Z5` is ACTIVE.
- Add as a lead, category *Large Retailer*, note expected 5,000 units per month.
- Submit – leads queue now shows the new entry for Sales team.

## 🚫 Mistakes To Avoid
- **Skipping GST verification** – will block later invoice creation.
- **Incorrect contact info** – leads to missed follow‑up.
- **Duplicate lead entry** – clutter the pipeline.

## ✅ SOP Checklist (Daily)
- [ ] Review target market list.
- [ ] Verify GST status for every new lead.
- [ ] Add notes and categorize leads.
- [ ] Submit leads to Sales Follow‑up.
- [ ] Sync with CRM Memory Brain.

## 📞 Escalation Flow
- **Invalid GST** → Notify **Technical Team**.
- **Lead quality issue** → Discuss with **Founder/Admin**.
- **Urgent high‑value lead** → Alert **Sales Follow‑up Team**.

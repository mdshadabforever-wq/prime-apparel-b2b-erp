# Content Team Guide

## 📌 Department Purpose
- **Responsibility:** Create and manage product content, images, descriptions, and marketing copy.
- **Goal:** Ensure product listings are accurate, attractive, and SEO‑optimized.
- **Business Importance:** Drives buyer interest and conversion rates.

## 🔐 Login Instructions
1. Open the ERP URL and log in.
2. After authentication you land on the **Content Dashboard**.
3. Role `CONTENT_TEAM` shows only content management menus.

## 📅 Daily Workflow
1. **Review Content Queue** – Navigate to **Content → Pending Updates**.
2. **Edit Product Page** – Click **Edit** next to a product, update:
   - Title, description (mix of Hindi & English), specifications.
   - Upload high‑resolution images (via Cloudinary integration).
3. **SEO Settings** – Add meta title, description, keywords.
4. **Publish** – Click **Save & Publish**; changes reflect instantly on the buyer portal.
5. **Version Log** – System records an audit entry for each edit.

## 🛠️ Real‑Life Example
- Update "Cotton T‑Shirt" description to include new fabric blend info.
- Upload three new angle photos.
- Add Hindi keywords "सुविधाजनक टी‑शर्ट".
- Save and publish – the product page now shows updated details.

## 🚫 Mistakes To Avoid
- **Missing image alt text** – harms SEO and accessibility.
- **Incorrect pricing** – always double‑check price fields; pricing is managed by Pricing Team.
- **Over‑writing approved copy** – respect version control.

## ✅ SOP Checklist (Daily)
- [ ] Pull pending content tasks.
- [ ] Verify image quality and SEO tags.
- [ ] Publish updates and verify on live site.
- [ ] Log any major copy changes.

## 📞 Escalation Flow
- **Content review conflict** → Notify **Marketing Team**.
- **Image upload failure** → Contact **Technical Team**.
- **Urgent buyer request** → Alert **Founder/Admin**.

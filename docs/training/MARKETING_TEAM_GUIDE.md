# Marketing Team Guide

## 📌 Department Purpose
- **Responsibility:** Plan and execute marketing campaigns, manage brand presence, generate leads.
- **Goal:** Drive qualified buyer traffic to the ERP portal and increase conversion rates.
- **Business Importance:** Directly fuels the sales pipeline and revenue growth.

## 🔐 Login Instructions
1. Open the ERP URL and log in with your corporate credentials.
2. After authentication you land on the **Marketing Dashboard**.
3. Role `MARKETING_TEAM` restricts view to campaign, lead, and analytics sections.

## 📅 Daily Workflow
1. **Check Campaign Performance** – Navigate to **Marketing → Campaign Overview**.
2. **Create New Campaign** – Click **New Campaign**, fill:
   - Campaign name, target audience, budget.
   - Select channels (email, social, WhatsApp – future integration).
3. **Launch & Monitor** – Press **Start**, then monitor real‑time KPIs (impressions, clicks, leads).
4. **Lead Management** – New leads appear under **Leads → New**, assign to Buyer Hunting team.
5. **Report Generation** – At day‑end, export **Campaign Report** (CSV) for analysis.

## 🛠️ Real‑Life Example
- Launch a “Winter Collection” email blast targeting 5,000 buyers.
- Set budget ₹50,000, schedule for 10 Oct.
- After launch, daily clicks rise to 1,200, leads to 150.
- Export report, share with Founder for ROI review.

## 🚫 Mistakes To Avoid
- **Incorrect target segment** – leads to low conversion.
- **Overspending budget** – violates financial controls.
- **Neglecting lead assignment** – delays sales follow‑up.

## ✅ SOP Checklist (Daily)
- [ ] Review yesterday’s campaign metrics.
- [ ] Approve any pending budget requests.
- [ ] Assign new leads to Buyer Hunting.
- [ ] Export and back up campaign reports.

## 📞 Escalation Flow
- **Budget overrun** → Alert **Accounts Team**.
- **Technical issue with campaign tool** → Contact **Technical Team**.
- **Urgent lead** → Notify **Sales Follow‑up Team**.

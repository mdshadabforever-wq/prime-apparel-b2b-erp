# Pricing Team Guide

## 📌 Department Purpose
- **Responsibility:** Define product pricing, manage discount rules, apply GST calculations.
- **Goal:** Maximize margin while staying competitive.
- **Business Importance:** Direct impact on revenue and profit margins.

## 🔐 Login Instructions
1. Open the ERP URL and log in with your corporate email/password.
2. After authentication you land on the **Pricing Dashboard**.
3. Role `PRICING_TEAM` limits view to pricing tables, discount configurator, and GST settings.

## 📅 Daily Workflow
1. **Review Market Data** – Navigate to **Pricing → Market Insights**.
2. **Update Price List** – Click **Edit Prices**, select product, modify base price.
3. **Set Discounts** – Open **Discounts** tab, define percentage or flat‑rate discounts per customer segment.
4. **GST Check** – System validates GST applicability for each product.
5. **Publish** – Click **Apply Changes**; a version‑controlled log is created.
6. **Audit** – Review **Pricing Change Log** for approvals.

## 🛠️ Real‑Life Example
- Need to increase price of “Premium Denim” by 5% due to raw‑material cost rise.
- Open product, adjust base price from ₹1200 to ₹1260.
- Add a 10% seasonal discount for bulk buyers.
- Save and publish – system auto‑updates all pending orders.

## 🚫 Mistakes To Avoid
- **Over‑discounting** – can erode profit.
- **Incorrect GST rate** – leads to compliance issues.
- **Publishing without audit** – may cause unauthorized price changes.

## ✅ SOP Checklist (Daily)
- [ ] Review latest market price alerts.
- [ ] Verify GST rates for all updated products.
- [ ] Approve or reject pending discount requests.
- [ ] Publish price changes before end‑of‑day.
- [ ] Log any manual overrides.

## 📞 Escalation Flow
- **Pricing conflict** → Alert **Founder/Admin** for final sign‑off.
- **GST mismatch** → Contact **Technical Team** to update tax tables.
- **System error** → Open a ticket with **Technical Support**.

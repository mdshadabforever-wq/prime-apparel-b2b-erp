# Accounts Team Guide

## 📌 Department Purpose
- **Responsibility:** Manage financial records, GST filing, invoicing reconciliation, and cash‑flow monitoring.
- **Goal:** Ensure accurate accounting, regulatory compliance, and transparent financial reporting.
- **Business Importance:** Direct impact on profit margins, tax liabilities, and stakeholder trust.

## 🔐 Login Instructions
1. Open the ERP URL and log in with your corporate email/password.
2. After authentication you land on the **Accounts Dashboard**.
3. Role `ACCOUNTS_TEAM` restricts view to ledgers, GST filings, and financial analytics.

## 📅 Daily Workflow
1. **Reconcile Invoices** – Navigate to **Accounts → Invoice Reconciliation**.
   - Match incoming payments with issued invoices.
   - Flag any mismatches for follow‑up.
2. **GST Filing** – Open **GST → Monthly Return**.
   - System auto‑populates taxable sales & purchases.
   - Verify amounts, then click **Submit** to the government portal.
3. **Cash‑Flow Review** – Check **Cash‑Flow Dashboard** for inflows/outflows.
4. **Financial Reporting** – Generate **Profit & Loss** and **Balance Sheet** reports (CSV/PDF).
5. **Audit Log Review** – Periodically review admin audit entries for any unauthorized financial changes.

## 🛠️ Real‑Life Example
- Received payment ₹1,00,000 for invoice #INV‑2026‑089.
- Reconcile by matching payment reference; system marks invoice as **Paid**.
- GST filing for March: system calculates GST payable ₹9,000, submit to portal.
- Export monthly profit‑loss report for Founder review.

## 🚫 Mistakes To Avoid
- **Missing payment reconciliation** – leads to overdue balances.
- **Incorrect GST figures** – may incur penalties.
- **Skipping audit log review** – can hide fraudulent activity.

## ✅ SOP Checklist (Daily)
- [ ] Verify all new payments against invoices.
- [ ] Update cash‑flow dashboard.
- [ ] Review any GST filing alerts.
- [ ] Export and back up financial reports.
- [ ] Scan audit logs for suspicious entries.

## 📞 Escalation Flow
- **Payment discrepancy** → Alert **Founder/Admin**.
- **GST filing error** → Contact **Technical Team** for API debugging.
- **Financial audit issue** → Engage **External Auditor**.

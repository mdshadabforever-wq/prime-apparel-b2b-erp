Build a complete notification and alert system for 
Prime Apparel Exports.

ALL NOTIFICATIONS ARE SENT VIA WHATSAPP to staff numbers.
Use WhatsApp Business API (same as customer bot).

NOTIFICATION TYPES AND TRIGGERS:

SALES NOTIFICATIONS:
→ Send to: Sales team WhatsApp

1. New hot lead registered
   Trigger: Lead score above 75
   Message: 
   "🔥 New HOT Lead!
   Name: [name]
   City: [city]
   Business: [type]
   Score: [X]/100
   Mobile: [number]
   
   Catalog bhej do abhi! 
   Dashboard: [link]"

2. New order received
   Trigger: Order confirmed in system
   Message:
   "🎉 New Order!
   Order: [ID]
   Buyer: [name], [city]
   Amount: ₹[amount]
   Items: [count] designs
   
   Inventory ko forward kar do.
   Dashboard: [link]"

3. Warm lead went inactive
   Trigger: Warm lead, no contact for 7 days
   Message:
   "⏰ Follow-up Needed!
   [Name] - [City]
   [Business type]
   Last contact: [X] days ago
   
   Ek WhatsApp bhejo aaj!"

INVENTORY NOTIFICATIONS:
→ Send to: Inventory team WhatsApp

4. Low stock alert
   Trigger: SKU qty drops below 10 pieces
   Message:
   "📦 Low Stock Alert!
   SKU: [code]
   Design: [name]
   Remaining: [X] pieces
   
   Purchase team ko inform karo.
   Reorder needed?"

5. New purchase order to receive
   Trigger: Supplier dispatch confirmed
   Message:
   "🚚 Maal Aa Raha Hai!
   Supplier: [name]
   Expected: [date]
   Designs: [count]
   Total pieces: [count]
   
   GRN + QC ready rakho!"

ACCOUNTS NOTIFICATIONS:
→ Send to: Accounts team WhatsApp

6. Payment due today
   Trigger: Every morning 9am, check due payments
   Message:
   "💰 Payment Due Today!
   [Buyer name] - [City]
   Order: [ID]
   Amount: ₹[amount]
   Due since: [date]
   
   Reminder bheja? Dashboard: [link]"

7. Payment overdue (7+ days late)
   Trigger: Payment 7 days past due date
   Message:
   "🔴 OVERDUE Payment!
   [Buyer name] - [City]
   Amount: ₹[amount]
   Overdue by: [X] days
   
   URGENT: Escalate to founder.
   No new orders to this buyer until cleared."

8. Payment received
   Trigger: Payment marked as received
   Message:
   "✅ Payment Received!
   [Buyer name]
   Amount: ₹[amount]
   Order: [ID]
   
   Accounts update karo!"

FOUNDER / ADMIN NOTIFICATIONS:
→ Send to: Founder WhatsApp

9. Daily summary (every evening 7pm)
   Message:
   "📊 Daily Report - [date]
   
   Orders Today: [count] | ₹[amount]
   New Leads: [count] (Hot: [x])
   Dispatched: [count] orders
   Payment Received: ₹[amount]
   Overdue Payments: [count]
   
   Full report: [dashboard link]"

10. Weekly summary (every Monday 9am)
    Message:
    "📈 Weekly Summary - Week [X]
    
    Revenue: ₹[amount] ([±%] vs last week)
    Orders: [count]
    New Buyers: [count]
    Repeat Orders: [X]%
    
    Top Seller: [SKU name]
    Needs Attention: [X overdue payments]
    
    Dashboard: [link]"

11. Critical alert (immediate)
    Triggers:
    - Any complaint received
    - Payment dispute
    - Very large order above ₹1 lakh
    - System error
    
    Message format varies by trigger.

BUYER NOTIFICATIONS (automated to buyers):
These go to buyers from the WhatsApp bot.

12. Order confirmation
13. Dispatch notification with LR
14. Payment reminder (3 days before due)
15. Payment overdue reminder
16. New catalog available (broadcast)
17. Seasonal offer announcement (broadcast)

NOTIFICATION SETTINGS:
- Admin can enable/disable each notification type
- Can change recipient numbers for each type
- Can change timing (immediate vs daily digest)
- Cannot disable critical alerts (overdue, complaints)
- Quiet hours: No non-critical notifications 
  between 10pm and 8am

NOTIFICATION LOG:
- All notifications logged in admin panel
- Show: type, recipient, time, delivery status
- Searchable and filterable
- Last 90 days history

CATEGORY: ORDERING
Q: Minimum order kitna hai?
A: Minimum 12 pieces per design hai. Alag alag 
   designs mein mix kar sakte hain, lekin ek 
   design mein minimum 12 pieces chahiye.

Q: Kya pieces mix kar sakte hain?
A: Haan, alag designs mix ho sakti hain, 
   lekin ek design ka minimum 12 pieces 
   hona zaroori hai.

Q: Sample piece milega?
A: Abhi hum single piece nahi bhejte. 
   Minimum 12 pieces se shuru hota hai.
   Lekin hamare catalog mein photos aur 
   videos hain jo quality clearly dikhate hain.

CATEGORY: PRICING
Q: Price negotiation hoti hai?
A: Hamari prices already competitive hain. 
   Bulk orders pe scheme milti hai:
   25-49 pcs: 3% discount
   50+ pcs: 5% discount
   Isse zyada discount nahi hoti kyunki 
   hum quality se compromise nahi karte.

Q: GST alag lagega?
A: Haan, GST applicable rates pe alag 
   lagega. Invoice mein clearly mention 
   hoga. GST registered buyers ke liye 
   input credit available hai.

CATEGORY: PAYMENT
Q: Payment kaise karni hai?
A: First order mein 100% advance payment.
   Options: Bank Transfer, NEFT, UPI.
   Details order confirm karte waqt share 
   kiye jaate hain.

Q: Credit milta hai?
A: Pehle 2 orders advance only.
   3rd order ke baad track record dekh ke 
   7 din credit consider kiya jaata hai.
   Credit GST registered aur regular buyers 
   ko hi milta hai.

Q: COD available hai?
A: COD available nahi hai B2B wholesale mein.
   Advance ya credit (eligible buyers ke liye) 
   only.

CATEGORY: DELIVERY
Q: Delivery kitne din mein aati hai?
A: Mumbai: 1-2 business days
   Maharashtra: 2-3 business days
   North India: 3-5 business days
   South India: 4-6 business days
   Northeast: 5-7 business days
   These are estimates, may vary.

Q: Transport charges kitne hain?
A: ₹500+ orders pe free delivery most locations.
   Exact amount order confirm karte waqt bataya 
   jaata hai based on weight and location.

Q: Tracking kaise karein?
A: Dispatch ke turant baad LR number WhatsApp 
   pe share kiya jaata hai.
   Transport company website pe track kar sakte 
   hain, ya hum se poochhein.

CATEGORY: QUALITY
Q: Quality guarantee hai?
A: Haan. Har lot 100% Mumbai mein QC hota hai.
   Photo mein jo dikhta hai wahi milta hai.
   Size consistency check hoti hai.
   Fabric aur stitching inspect hoti hai.

Q: Agar quality sahi na ho?
A: Delivery ke 48 ghante ke andar photo ke 
   saath report karo.
   Manufacturing defect pe replacement ya 
   credit note milega.
   Kisi bhi issue pe WhatsApp karo turant.

Q: Return policy kya hai?
A: Manufacturing defects: Full replacement
   Damaged in transit: Transport claim + 
   replacement support
   Wrong item sent: Free replacement + 
   return pickup
   Buyer change of mind: Not accepted 
   (wholesale policy)

CATEGORY: REGISTRATION
Q: Registration kyun zaroori hai?
A: Wholesale prices sirf registered verified 
   buyers ko show hoti hain.
   Registration se dedicated service milti hai.
   Order history, invoices, account management 
   sab available hota hai.

Q: Registration kitne din mein approve hoti hai?
A: Usually 24 hours mein. 
   Business details verify karte hain.
   Approval ya rejection WhatsApp pe bataya 
   jaata hai.

CATEGORY: PRODUCTS
Q: Kaunse fabrics available hain?
A: Currently available:
   - Cotton (daily wear, summer)
   - Rayon (year-round)
   - Georgette (festive, party)
   - Crepe (office, semi-formal)
   - Cotton Linen blend (premium daily)
   New fabrics regularly add hote hain.

Q: Seasonal collections kab aati hain?
A: Summer cotton: February-March
   Festive (Navratri/Diwali): July-August
   Wedding season: October-November
   Winter collection: September-October
   WhatsApp list mein rehne par 
   pehle inform kiya jaata hai.

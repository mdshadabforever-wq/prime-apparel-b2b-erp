Build a complete admin dashboard for Prime Apparel Exports 
internal management system.

WHO USES THIS:
- Founder / Owner: sees everything
- Sales team: sees buyers, leads, orders
- Inventory team: sees stock only
- Accounts team: sees orders, payments, cash flow

DASHBOARD SECTIONS:

1. TOP KPI BAR (visible to all roles)
Show live numbers:
- Today's orders received
- Today's revenue
- This month's revenue
- Active buyers count
- Hot leads waiting
- Low stock alerts
- Overdue payments count
- Pending dispatches

Each KPI is clickable — goes to detailed view.

2. MAIN DASHBOARD TABS:

TAB 1 — OVERVIEW (Founder only)
- Revenue chart: last 30 days bar chart
- Orders chart: last 30 days line chart
- Top 5 selling SKUs this month
- Top 5 buyers by order value this month
- Stock health summary:
  Available | Low Stock | Out of Stock count
- Cash position:
  Bank balance (manual entry) | 
  Receivables | Payables

TAB 2 — ORDERS
Table with filters: Date / Status / Buyer / Amount
Columns: Order ID | Date | Buyer | Items | Amount | 
         Payment | Status | Action
Actions: View details | Mark dispatched | 
         Generate invoice | Track shipment
Color coding:
- Green row: Paid + dispatched
- Yellow row: Paid, pending dispatch
- Orange row: Dispatched, payment pending
- Red row: Overdue payment

TAB 3 — BUYERS
Table with search and filters
Columns: Name | City | Business Type | Score | 
         Status | Last Order | Total Value | Action
Filters: Hot/Warm/Cold | Active/Pending | City
Actions: View profile | Add note | Change status |
         Send WhatsApp | View order history

Buyer profile page shows:
- All their details from registration
- Score card breakdown
- Complete order history
- WhatsApp conversation log
- Notes timeline
- Payment history

TAB 4 — STOCK
Table: SKU | Design | Fabric | Available | 
       Reserved | Price | Status
Filters: Category | Fabric | Status | Low stock only
Actions: Update qty | Edit price | View history |
         Mark discontinued

Quick actions:
- "Low Stock" button: shows all items below 10 pcs
- "Dead Stock" button: shows items with no sale in 60 days

TAB 5 — LEADS
Table: Name | Mobile | Source | Score | Status | 
       Last Contact | Action
Filters: Hot/Warm/Cold | Source | Date range
Actions: Convert to buyer | Assign to staff |
         Send WhatsApp | Add note | Mark lost

Daily lead report visible:
New leads today: X
Contacted today: X  
Converted this week: X

TAB 6 — ANALYTICS
(Founder and admin only)
- Monthly revenue trend (12 months)
- Category-wise sales pie chart
- City-wise buyer heatmap
- Repeat vs new buyer ratio
- Average order value trend
- Best performing designs
- Supplier performance comparison
- Margin analysis by category

TAB 7 — CASH FLOW
Accounts team access:
- Income vs expense bar chart (monthly)
- Receivables aging:
  0-7 days | 7-30 days | 30-60 days | 60+ days
- Payment due calendar
- Quick entry: Add income / Add expense
- Export to Excel button

TAB 8 — WHATSAPP CENTER
All WhatsApp conversations:
- Inbox: all active conversations
- Hot: conversations needing immediate reply
- Bot handled: conversations AI resolved
- Escalated: conversations waiting for human
- Quick reply templates
- Broadcast: send message to buyer group

3. NOTIFICATIONS PANEL (top right)
Real-time alerts:
🔴 New hot lead registered
🟡 Order overdue for dispatch
🔴 Payment overdue: [buyer] - [amount]
🟡 Stock low: [SKU] - only [X] pieces left
🟢 Payment received: [buyer] - [amount]
🟢 New order confirmed: [buyer] - [amount]

4. QUICK ACTIONS BAR
- Add new order (manual)
- Add stock entry
- Send WhatsApp broadcast
- Generate invoice
- Mark payment received

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS:
Admin: Everything
Founder: Everything
Sales: Buyers, Leads, Orders (view only), 
       WhatsApp center
Inventory: Stock tab, Purchase orders
Accounts: Cash flow, Invoices, Payments
Content: No dashboard, separate content panel

DESIGN:
- Dark sidebar with icon navigation
- Clean white content area
- Mobile responsive
- Color coded status everywhere
- Load fast — no heavy charts on mobile

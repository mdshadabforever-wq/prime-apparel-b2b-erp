Build a buyer registration page for Prime Apparel Exports 
B2B wholesale website.

PURPOSE:
New buyers fill this form to get access to catalog and 
pricing. Company manually approves each buyer. Approved 
buyers get login credentials.

FORM FIELDS — All Required Unless Marked Optional:

SECTION 1 — PERSONAL DETAILS
- Full Name
- Mobile Number (WhatsApp number — this becomes username)
- Email Address (optional)
- Password (create for login)
- Confirm Password

SECTION 2 — BUSINESS DETAILS
- Business / Shop Name
- Business Type dropdown:
  - Offline Retail Shop
  - Online Seller (Meesho/Instagram/Website)
  - Boutique
  - Mini-Wholesaler
  - Other
- GST Number (optional but show benefit text: 
  "GST buyers get faster credit approval")
- Years in business: dropdown (Less than 1 / 1-3 / 3-5 / 5+)

SECTION 3 — LOCATION
- City
- State dropdown (all Indian states)
- Pin Code
- Full address (optional)
- Google Maps link to shop (optional)

SECTION 4 — ONLINE PRESENCE
- Instagram page link (optional)
- Facebook page link (optional)
- Website link (optional)
- JustDial listing link (optional)

SECTION 5 — BUYING INTEREST
- Which products interested in? 
  (multi-select checkboxes)
  Cotton Kurtis / Rayon Kurtis / Suit Sets / 
  Festive Wear / Daily Wear
- Expected monthly purchase quantity:
  dropdown: 50-100 / 100-500 / 500-1000 / 1000+
- Currently buying from: (optional text field)
- How did you hear about us:
  Instagram / WhatsApp / Google / Reference / Other

AFTER SUBMISSION:
- Show success message: 
  "Registration received! Our team will review and 
  WhatsApp you within 24 hours."
- Auto-send WhatsApp message to company's number 
  with all registration details
- Show buyer's registered WhatsApp number clearly
- Give option to directly WhatsApp: 
  "Track your registration on WhatsApp"

ADMIN SIDE:
- All registrations go to an admin panel
- Each registration shows calculated score 0-100 based on:
  - Business type (15 points)
  - Online presence links submitted (15 points)
  - Location tier (10 points)
  - Purchase quantity expected (15 points)
  - GST registered (10 points)
- Admin can Approve / Reject / Request More Info
- Approved buyers get WhatsApp notification with 
  login link automatically

DESIGN:
- Multi-step form — not one long page
- Show progress bar: Step 1 of 5
- Each step has clear heading and minimal fields
- Mobile friendly — easy to fill on phone
- Trust signals visible: "Your data is safe. 
  We never share your information."

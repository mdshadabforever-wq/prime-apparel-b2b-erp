Build an automated lead scoring system for Prime Apparel 
Exports that scores every new contact from 0-100.

SCORING FACTORS AND WEIGHTS:

Factor 1 — Business Type (15 points)
- Established offline shop with fixed location: 15
- Active boutique with online presence: 12
- Online seller with good following: 10
- Small part-time reseller: 6
- Only WhatsApp group seller, no structure: 3
- Unknown / not specified: 5

Factor 2 — Online Presence (15 points)
Check their Instagram, Facebook links if provided:
- Google listing + active Instagram (recent posts): 15
- Only Google listing OR only one active social: 10
- Weak presence, few posts, small following: 6
- No online presence found: 3
- Did not share any links: 5

Factor 3 — Location Stability (10 points)
- Shop in main market, Tier 1 or 2 city: 10
- Tier 3 town, shop looks stable: 7
- Village or remote area: 4
- No fixed address, unclear location: 2
- Metro city residential area (not commercial): 6

Factor 4 — Activity Level (15 points)
Check their social media activity:
- Posts daily or every 2 days, customer photos: 15
- Posts weekly, some engagement: 10
- Posts monthly or less: 5
- Account exists but dormant: 2

Factor 5 — Product Fit (20 points)
- Currently sells ethnic kurtis and suits primarily: 20
- Sells mix of ethnic and western: 14
- Mostly western but some ethnic: 8
- Completely different category (lingerie, kids): 2
- Online seller with no specific category: 10

Factor 6 — Purchase Potential (15 points)
Based on their stated expected purchase quantity:
- 500+ pieces per month: 15
- 200-499 pieces per month: 12
- 100-199 pieces per month: 8
- 50-99 pieces per month: 5
- Less than 50 pieces: 2

Factor 7 — Trust Signals (10 points)
- GST registered, shared GST number: 10
- GST registered, not shared yet: 7
- Not GST registered but business established: 5
- New buyer, no documents yet: 4
- Evasive about business details: 1

TOTAL: 100 points

SCORE CATEGORIES:
75-100 = HOT LEAD (Green)
  → Full catalog access immediately
  → Personal call from sales within 2 hours
  → Eligible for field visit
  → Special first-order scheme offer
  → Add to high priority follow-up queue

50-74 = WARM LEAD (Amber)
  → Limited catalog (top 20 designs only)
  → WhatsApp nurture sequence starts
  → Call within 24 hours
  → No field visit until they show strong interest
  → Standard follow-up queue

Below 50 = COLD LEAD (Red)
  → Broadcast only (general catalog)
  → No personal follow-up from sales team
  → No field staff engagement
  → Auto nurture drip only
  → Re-evaluate if they show interest

SCORE CALCULATION:
- Score auto-calculates when buyer registers
- Score updates when more data is added
- Score shown in admin panel with color coding
- Score shown to sales team with recommendation

SCORE CARD DISPLAY:
Show breakdown like:
Business Type: 12/15
Online Presence: 10/15
Location: 7/10
Activity Level: 8/15
Product Fit: 20/20
Purchase Potential: 8/15
Trust Signals: 7/10
TOTAL: 72/100 — WARM LEAD ⬛

ACTIONS BASED ON SCORE:
When score calculated, automatically:
1. Assign to correct lead bucket
2. Add to appropriate WhatsApp sequence
3. Notify relevant sales staff
4. Schedule follow-up date in system
